Planning aid, not for navigation. These are the regular astronomical tides, worked out from harmonic constants fitted to tide gauge records. Wind, air pressure, swell and river flow are not included and can shift real high water by tens of minutes and heights by half a metre or more, especially in storms. Check the Admiralty EasyTide service or a local tide table before any trip where timing matters for safety.

Tide data: TICON-4 harmonic constants (Hart-Davis, Dettmering and Seitz, 2025, doi:10.17882/109129, licensed CC BY 4.0), supplied through the Neaps tide database. Predictions use the Neaps tide predictor (MIT licence). Heights are metres above chart datum (lowest astronomical tide). Moon phase comes from a standard low-precision lunar series.

Place search uses OpenStreetMap Nominatim (© OpenStreetMap contributors). Port names are matched in your browser; when you press Search, the text you typed is sent to Nominatim to find the place.
